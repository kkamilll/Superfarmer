📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal

Be the first to have at least 1 of each: 🐰 Rabbit, 🐑 Sheep, 🐷 Pig, 🐮 Cow, 🐴 Horse.

🎲 On Your Turn

  1. Click ROLL DICE (you can also trade before or after rolling).
  2. Breeding: Add your farm animals + dice of that species. Every full pair (÷ 2) gives +1 new animal from the bank.
  3. Click END TURN when ready.

🐺 Predators & Dogs

🐺 Wolf

Eats all animals except 🐴 Horses. Blocked by 🐩 Big Dog.

🦊 Fox

Eats all 🐰 Rabbits. Blocked by 🐕 Small Dog.
