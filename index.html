📖 Game Rules — Super Farmer Pro

🎯 Objective

Be the first farmer to collect at least 1 animal of each of the 5 main species on your farm: 🐰 Rabbit, 🐑 Sheep, 🐷 Pig, 🐮 Cow, and 🐴 Horse.

🎲 Turn Flow & Herd Breeding

  1. Phase 1 (Before the roll): You may execute any available trades on the Market, then click ROLL DICE.
  2. Herd Breeding: Count all animals of the rolled species on your farm + the dice. For every full pair (sum ÷ 2), you receive 1 new animal from the Bank Herd and add it to your farm!
    💡 Breeding Examples:
    • You have 0 🐰 + roll 2 🐰 on dice = 2 total (1 pair) ➔ you get +1 🐰 (you now have 1).
    • You have 1 🐰 + roll 1 🐰 on die = 2 total (1 pair) ➔ you get +1 🐰 (you now have 2).
    • You have 3 🐰 + roll 1 🐰 on die = 4 total (2 pairs) ➔ you get +2 🐰 (you now have 5).
    • You have 4 🐰 + roll 2 🐰 on dice = 6 total (3 pairs) ➔ you get +3 🐰 (you now have 7).
  3. Phase 2 (After the roll): After collecting newborn animals, you may make further trades. When finished, click END TURN.

🐺 Predators — Wolf and Fox

🐺 Wolf (Die 1)

Devours all animals from your farm except Horses and Big Dogs! Lost animals return to the bank.

🐩 Big Dog repels the wolf (the dog is lost and returns to bank, herd is saved).
🦊 Fox (Die 2)

Devours all your Rabbits 🐰 (other species are completely safe). Rabbits return to the bank.

🐕 Small Dog repels the fox (the dog is lost and returns to bank, rabbits are saved).
